# Glossary

**Board**: The 7x7 grid of cells where tiles are placed.

**Cell**: One square of the board. It is empty or holds one tile.

**Tile**: A square piece with 4 edges and two faces. Players draw, place and discard tiles.

**Face**: One of the two sides of a tile. The player chooses which face is up when placing. A face holds 1 or 2 parts.

**Edge**: One of the 4 sides of a tile. An edge shows a part or background.

**Part**: A piece of an animal drawn on a face. One of Head, Tail, Straight body or Turn body, and always of one species.

**Head**: A part that touches 1 edge. It ends an animal.

**Tail**: A part that touches 1 edge. It ends an animal.

**Straight body**: A part that touches 2 opposite edges.

**Turn body**: A part that touches 2 adjacent edges.

**Background**: An edge with no part.

**Species**: Cat or Dog.

**Animal**: A connected chain of parts of one species, across one or more tiles. An animal is open until it is complete.

**Complete animal**: An animal with exactly 1 head and 1 tail.
