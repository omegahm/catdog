# Glossary

**Board**: The 7x7 grid of cells where tiles are placed.

**Cell**: One square of the board. It is empty or holds one tile.

**Tile**: A square piece with 4 edges and two faces. Players draw, place and discard tiles.

**Face**: One of the two sides of a tile. The player chooses which face is up when placing. A face holds 1 or 2 parts.

**Edge**: One of the 4 sides of a tile. An edge shows a part or background.

**Part**: A piece of an animal drawn on a face. One of Head, Tail, Straight body or Turn body, and always of one species.

**Head**: A part that touches 1 edge. It ends an animal.

**Tail**: A part that touches 1 edge. It ends an animal.

**Straight body**: A part that touches 2 opposite edges.

**Turn body**: A part that touches 2 adjacent edges.

**Background**: An edge with no part.

**Species**: Cat or Dog.

**Animal**: A connected chain of parts of one species, across one or more tiles. An animal is open until it is complete.

**Complete animal**: An animal with exactly 1 head and 1 tail. It belongs to the player who plays as its species, no matter who completed it.

**Length**: The number of parts in an animal, counted from tail to head. A tile that the animal passes through twice adds two parts.

**Player species**: The species a player plays as for the whole game. It is set by the first complete animal.

**Discard pile**: The open stack of tiles that players drew but did not place. It goes back to the bag after each reserve action.

**Reserve**: A place next to a player that holds 0 or 1 tile. A player may play their reserve tile instead of drawing.

**Reserve action**: What a player gets once per placement that completes one or more animals: take or swap one tile from the discard pile into their reserve, or skip.

**Length point**: The 1 point a player scores for a length when they have more complete animals of that length than the opponent.

**Tiebreak**: When points are tied, the player who has more animals at the longest length where the counts are different wins. If no such length exists, the game is a draw.
