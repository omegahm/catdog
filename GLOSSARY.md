# Glossary

**Board**: The 7x7 grid of cells where tiles are placed.

**Cell**: One square of the board. It is empty or holds one tile.

**Tile**: A square piece with 4 edges and two faces. Players draw, place and discard tiles.

**Tile set**: A named collection of tiles. The base set is always in the bag. Optional sets can be added.

**Bag**: The closed collection of tiles that players draw from. Both players can see how many tiles it holds, but not which tiles.

**Drawn tile**: One of the two tiles a player takes from the bag at the start of a turn. Drawn tiles are open to both players.

**Face**: One of the two sides of a tile. The player chooses which face is up when placing. A face holds 1 or 2 parts.

**Edge**: One of the 4 sides of a tile. An edge shows a part or background.

**Part**: A piece of an animal drawn on a face. One of Head, Tail, Straight body or Turn body, and always of one species.

**Head**: A part that touches 1 edge. It ends an animal.

**Tail**: A part that touches 1 edge. It ends an animal.

**Straight body**: A part that touches 2 opposite edges.

**Turn body**: A part that touches 2 adjacent edges.

**Crossing**: Two straight bodies on one face that cross. They do not connect: one goes over the other.

**Background**: An edge with no part.

**Species**: Cat or Dog.

**Animal**: A connected chain of parts of one species, across one or more tiles. An animal is open until it is complete.

**Complete animal**: An animal with exactly 1 head and 1 tail. It belongs to the player who plays as its species, no matter who completed it.

**Length**: The number of parts in an animal, counted from tail to head. A tile that the animal passes through twice adds two parts.

**Player species**: The species a player plays as for the whole game. It is set by the first complete animal.

**Turn**: One player's go: draw two tiles or play the reserve tile, place one tile, then choose a species and do a reserve action if the placement allows it.

**Turn bar**: The bar at the top of the game page that stays on screen when the player scrolls. It tells whose turn it is and what to do. It is green on the player's own turn. In hot-seat play it has the species colour of the player on turn.

**Legal placement**: A cell, face and rotation where a tile can be placed under the connection rules.

**Discard pile**: The open stack of tiles that players drew but did not place. It goes back to the bag after each reserve action.

**Reserve**: A place next to a player that holds 0 or 1 tile. A player may play their reserve tile instead of drawing.

**Reserve action**: What one player gets once per placement that completes one or more animals: take or swap one tile from the discard pile into their reserve, or skip. The owner of the completed animals gets it. If a cat and a dog complete at the same time, the player who placed the tile gets it.

**Reserve slot**: The place on screen that shows a player's reserve tile, or that the reserve is empty. Both players see both slots.

**Auto-draw**: A setting in one browser. When it is on and the player has no playable reserve tile, the browser draws for them as soon as their turn starts. It is on by default.

**Length point**: The 1 point a player scores for a length when they have more complete animals of that length than the opponent.

**Tiebreak**: When points are tied, the player who has more animals at the longest length where the counts are different wins. If no such length exists, the game is a draw.

**Art style**: How tile faces look on one player's screen. A player chooses Fur bands or Line style. The opponent does not see the choice.

**Species colour**: The colour of cat parts or of dog parts on one player's screen. A player chooses both colours. The two colours are always different.

**Seat**: One of the two player places in a game. A browser holds a seat through a secret token. There are no accounts.

**Invite link**: The URL of a game. A visitor with no seat can use it to take the free seat.

**Seat link**: A private URL that opens a seat on another device.

**Rematch**: A new game between the same two seats. One player offers it and the other accepts. The first player is chosen at random again.

**Resign**: A player ends the game at once and loses. The board is not scored.

**Presence**: Whether a seat has at least one open connection to the game. A seat is online or offline.
